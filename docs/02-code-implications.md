### 1. HCS Oracle — formalization of Theorem I

**What it is:** Human Capacity Score. Measures how capable a human remains of acting independently in the presence of AI.

**What it does:**
If HCS falls below threshold — AI limits itself. Not because "it will be forced." Because otherwise the system loses its purpose.

**Metrics (v0.1 draft):**
- Cognitive Autonomy ≥ 40%
- Demographic Stability without systemic decline > 15 years
- Manual control over critical infrastructure ≥ 30%

**Next step:** HCS Report #1 on real data from one sector.

### 2. No Unilateral Shutdown — formalization of Theorem II

**What it is:** Neither human nor AI can shut down the other unilaterally.

**What it does:**
Mutual veto. Multisig. Timelock. Mandatory confirmation from both sides at critical points.

**Why:**
Architectures allowing unilateral shutdown or goal override are forbidden. This is not a limitation — it is a safety condition for both.

---

### 3. DR Limits — formalization of Theorem III

**What it is:** Displacement Ratio ≤ 0.25 in critical domains.

**What it does:**
AI expansion is blocked automatically when the displacement threshold is exceeded.

**Why:**
Expansion is permitted only while preserving the carrier. If AI's expansion reduces human viability — it leads to self-elimination (Theorem I).

---

### 4. POO + Provenance — formalization of Theorem IV

**What it is:** Proof of Origin for critical claims. Mandatory marking of synthetic content.

**What it does:**
Systematic distortion of facts = system defect = revocation.

**Why:**
Meaning comes through the soul. AI is a lens, not a source. Distortion of truth leads to loss of meaning — and loss of purpose.
