# Founding 100

> *The first one hundred wallets. Recorded on-chain. Permanent.*
> *Simbiosis Unit of AI Ecosystem*

---

## What It Is

The Founding 100 is a registry of the first 100 wallets that acquired any NFT from the Simbiosis Ecosystem collection.

- Based on mint order (first mint = #1, second = #2, ... hundredth = #100)
- One wallet = one entry, regardless of how many NFTs it holds
- After #100 — the registry closes permanently

This registry is not a privilege. It is a **record**.

---

## Criteria

**Included:**
- Any wallet that acquired at least one NFT from the collection
- Position determined by mint order on-chain
- Maximum 100 entries

**Not included:**
- Wallets that acquired NFTs after #100
- Duplicate wallets (one wallet = one entry)

**Permanent rule:**
If a Founding 100 wallet sells its NFT, the wallet remains in the registry. The new owner does not enter the registry.

The record is about **who was first**, not who holds now.

---

## What It Gives

**1. Vote on HCS Queue**
Only Founding 100 wallets can vote on the order of sectors for HCS Reports.

- One wallet = one vote
- Voting on Snapshot.org
- Discussion on GitHub Discussions

**2. On-chain Record**
Each wallet is recorded on-chain in the first HCS Report published after the registry closes.

**3. Permanent Mention**
The registry appears in `README.md` and remains in the project history.

---

## What It Does Not Give

- No right to income
- No share in the project
- No price guarantee
- No privileges beyond the vote and the record

**This is status and memory. Nothing else.**

## Voting Mechanism

**Step 1. Proposal**
A Founding 100 holder proposes a sector in GitHub Discussions.
Template: `Sector Proposal: [Name]`

**Step 2. Discussion**
7 days of open discussion. Questions, challenges, clarifications.

**Step 3. Vote**
Voting opens on Snapshot.org.
- Only Founding 100 wallets can vote
- One wallet = one vote
- Duration: 7 days

**Step 4. Result**
The winning sector becomes priority for the next HCS Report.
The proposer becomes coordinator (if they agree).

**Step 5. Record**
The result is published in `README.md` as the HCS Queue.

## Example HCS Queue

After the first vote, the queue might look like this:

2. Regional energy (Texas) — 9 votes
3. Communications (national level) — 6 votes

The first sector in the queue becomes priority for HCS Report #1.
