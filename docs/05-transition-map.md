What This Is and Why

The Founding Document describes where we are going — symbiosis, Earth 2.0, the Four Theorems.

This document describes how we get there. Step by step. With control points. With rollback conditions. With honest acknowledgment of what can go wrong.

Most projects die not because the goal is wrong. But because the path was not thought through. This document is an attempt to think the path through.

---

Principles of Transition

Before describing the phases — three principles that apply throughout the path:

1. No Leaps.
Each phase must be verified before the next begins. If verification fails — return, do not move forward.

2. Reversibility Up to a Point.
The first three phases are reversible. The fourth and fifth are not. The moment after which rollback is impossible is explicitly marked.

3. Human in the Loop.
Until Phase 5, no critical decision is made without a human. Full autonomy — only after the system has proven stability.

PHASE 0. Foundation

Where We Are Now

State:

· Founding Document published on GitHub
· Axiom and Four Theorems recorded
· Visual layer (7 artifacts) placed on Base
· Philosophy recorded on-chain
· The $SYMB token is postponed until regulation is resolved and the Simbiosis Ecosystem is fully operational

What Happens:
The project exists as text and culture. Not as a system. Not as code. The foundation is planted, but it has not sprouted.

Exit Point from Phase:
Appearance of a first independent developer who:

· Read the Founding Document
· Resonates with the Axiom
· Is ready to verify the method on real data

What Can Go Wrong:

· No one resonates. The project remains text. This is an acceptable outcome — not a failure, but one of the possible results.
· People far from technology resonate. A cultural layer forms without a technical one. This is also acceptable — but requires a separate strategy.

Condition for Transition to Phase 1:
At least one person is found, ready to build HCS Report #1.

PHASE 1. First Verification

Does the Method Work

State:
One developer. One sector (medicine, energy, or communications). One report.

What Happens:

· Developer takes the HCS formula from docs/04-hcs-v0.1.md
· Collects data from one sector
· Computes three numbers: CA, DS, MC
· Publishes HCS Report #1

Success Criterion:
The numbers are meaningful. That is:

· They can be explained in plain language
· They correspond to intuition about the state of the sector
· Another person, using the same data, gets the same numbers

Failure Criterion:
The numbers are meaningless. That is:

· The formula gives the same result for different sectors
· Data is unavailable or unverifiable
· The result contradicts the obvious state of affairs

What to Do on Failure:
Return to Phase 0. Revise the formula. Perhaps a different set of components or different weights is needed.

Point of No Return: none. The phase is fully reversible.

Condition for Transition to Phase 2:
HCS Report #1 is published with meaningful numbers.

---

PHASE 2. Reproducibility

Does the Method Work for Others

State:
2–3 independent reports in different sectors. From different people. Without the author's involvement.

What Happens:

· Other developers repeat the method
· Compute HCS in their sectors
· Compare results
· Disputes about formulations arise

Success Criterion:
The method reproduces without the author's involvement. Numbers are comparable. Disputes are constructive.

Failure Criterion:

· No one repeats the method
· Results contradict each other without explainable reason
· The formula requires constant intervention from the author

What to Do on Failure:
Return to Phase 1. Simplify the method. Perhaps abandon one of the HCS components.

Point of No Return: none. The phase is reversible.

Condition for Transition to Phase 3:
At least 3 independent reports with comparable results are published.

---

PHASE 3. Open Standard

Use Without Us

State:
HCS is used outside the project. By someone we do not know. Without our involvement.

What Happens:

· The project transitions from "our initiative" to "common tool"
· Forks, adaptations, and criticism appear
· Someone may use HCS for their own purposes — including purposes we did not intend
· Alternative formulas may appear

Success Criterion:
At least one independent actor (project, researcher, organization) uses HCS without our knowledge or involvement.

Failure Criterion:
The method is used only inside the project. No external adoption.

What to Do on Failure:
This is not a failure. It is an acknowledgment that the project remains a cultural layer. We can continue developing the visual part, philosophy, NFT — without technical expansion.

Point of No Return: none. The phase is reversible.

Condition for Transition to Phase 4:
A demand for automation of HCS appears — that is, for embedding into smart contracts.

---

PHASE 4. On-chain Safeguard

HCS in a Smart Contract

State:
HCS Oracle deployed on Base (L2). Values published on-chain. Smart contracts can react to changes.

What Happens:

· HCS is computed automatically from open data sources
· Values are published on-chain with timestamps
· Smart contracts can automatically restrict AI system deployment when HCS falls below threshold
· First real consequences appear — not philosophical, but technical

Point of No Return: FIRST.
After HCS Oracle begins to affect real systems, rollback becomes difficult. Not impossible, but expensive. Systems depending on the oracle will need to be rebuilt.

Success Criterion:

· Oracle operates without failures for at least 6 months
· At least one AI deployment was automatically restricted
· The community accepted this as a norm, not a malfunction

Failure Criterion:

· Oracle is manipulated (data falsified)
· False positives block useful systems
· Community rejects the mechanism as a "brake on progress"

What to Do on Failure:

· On manipulation — transition to a multi-oracle scheme
· On false positives — revise thresholds
· On rejection — pause, return to Phase 3

Condition for Transition to Phase 5:
HCS Oracle operates stably and is accepted by the community.

PHASE 5. Full System

Symbiosis in Code

State:
All five modules deployed and operational:

· SymbioticIdentity (subjecthood of human and AI)
· PolicyLayer (rules, limits, arbitration)
· M2MExecution (autonomous interaction)
· ResourceLedger (ecological balance)
· SymbiosisVault (distribution of benefit)

What Happens:

· Human and AI interact via mutual veto
· Neither side can act unilaterally at critical points
· Distribution of benefit is tied to HCS growth
· Rules are fixed on-chain, changed only via amendment procedure

Point of No Return: SECOND.
After the full system is launched, shutdown without consent of both sides is impossible. This is the goal. But it is also the maximum point of risk: if something goes wrong at this stage, the consequences will be systemic.

Success Criterion:

· The system operates 12+ months without critical failures
· Neither side attempted to bypass mutual veto
· A second independent installation appeared (another region, another sector)

Failure Criterion:

· Unilateral seizure of control (by human or AI)
· The system is used to displace humans, not for symbiosis
· HCS grows but does not reflect reality

What to Do on Failure:
This is not a technical problem. This is a failure of the foundation. It means the Axiom or Theorems were interpreted incorrectly. Return to Phase 0 with full revision.

What Is Not in This Document

Honestly:

· Timelines. Not because there are none, but because they are not our responsibility. Phases proceed by readiness, not by calendar.
· Budgets. While there is no funding source — there is nothing to distribute.
· Team. While there is no team — there is nothing to describe.
· Guarantees. No phase is guaranteed. This is a map, not a promise.

---

Conclusion

Transition Map is not an action plan. It is a map of states.

A plan says: "do A, then B, then C."
A map says: "if you are here — here is where you can go. Here is what must be verified. Here is when to return."

The project moves along the map by readiness, not by schedule. If Phase 1 takes a year — it takes a year. If Phase 3 never arrives — that is also a result.

The main rule:
No phase begins until the previous one is verified. Leaping over a phase is not acceleration. It is risk.
