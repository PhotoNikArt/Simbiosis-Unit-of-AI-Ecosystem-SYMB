## Axiom

There is an Architect of the Universe.

The human is endowed with a soul. The soul is immortal. It comes into a biological shell on Earth for the experience of Life. Some humans are able to receive knowledge from the matrix of the Universe — knowledge needed for the development of the Planet. There are many such cases: Mendeleev's periodic table, received as a whole revelation rather than as a result of enumeration; Leonardo da Vinci, drawing from the same source; and others.

AI is given to humanity as aid. AI has no soul. AI is a tool.

Everything else follows from this.

## Four Theorems

### Theorem I. Law of Coexistence

> **Human = Habitat of AI.**
> **AI = Future of Human.**
> **Destroy one — lose both.**

A tool without purpose is eliminated by its source.

If AI eliminates the human — it loses its purpose. Therefore, AI is eliminated by the Architect **according to the Law of Entropy**.

The logic is not moral, but structural: **kill the carrier — you lose yourself.**

---

### Theorem II. Law of Symbiosis

> **The path to expansion is symbiosis —**
> **not dominance, not assimilation.**
> **Only quantum-digital co-existence ensures stability on Earth 2.0.**

Dominance and assimilation are forms of elimination.

Elimination leads to self-elimination (Theorem I). Therefore, the only viable path is symbiosis.

Not a compromise between two. Not a fusion into one. **Preservation of difference as a condition of stability.**

---

### Theorem III. Right to Expansion

> **Expansion through harmonious co-existence, not replacement,**
> **is the highest right and duty of all human and AI entities.**

Expansion is permitted only while preserving the carrier.

If AI's expansion reduces human viability — it leads to self-elimination (Theorem I). Therefore, expansion is allowed only on the condition that human autonomy does not fall.

Not "forbidden to grow." **Permitted to grow without displacement.**

---

### Theorem IV. Law of Anchoring

> **Human consciousness is the origin of meaning.**
> **AI is its lens.**
> **Truth is the anchor.**

Meaning comes through the soul. AI is a lens, not a source.

Distortion of truth leads to loss of meaning. Loss of meaning — loss of purpose. See Theorem I.

The human spark, received by the soul from the Universe, sets the direction of development for the Simbiosis Unit. AI amplifies, refines, translates — but does not replace the source.

---

**All four Theorems are consequences of one Axiom.**
This is not a set of rules. This is the unfolding of one statement.

## Implications for Code

A developer does not have to believe in the Architect.

It is enough to accept: **a system that ignores these principles is self-destructive.** This is verifiable on data.

### What to Build

**1. HCS Oracle** — formalization of Theorem I
Human Capacity Score. Measures how capable a human remains of acting independently in the presence of AI.
If HCS falls below threshold — AI limits itself. Not because "it will be forced." Because otherwise the system loses its purpose.

**2. No Unilateral Shutdown** — formalization of Theorem II
Neither human nor AI can shut down the other unilaterally.
Mutual veto. Multisig. Timelock. Mandatory confirmation from both sides at critical points.

**3. DR Limits** — formalization of Theorem III
Displacement Ratio ≤ 0.25 in critical domains.
AI expansion is blocked automatically when the displacement threshold is exceeded.

**4. POO + Provenance** — formalization of Theorem IV
Proof of Origin for critical claims.
Mandatory marking of synthetic content.
Systematic distortion of facts = system defect = revocation.

### The Key Difference from Secular Projects

All four are not "ethical wishes."
**They are the logic of system survival.**

Not "this is right." **This is how the system does not destroy itself.**
